Privacy policy

Hill Roller for iPhone · Last updated 2 November 2026

The short version

  • Hill Roller has no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or anything else about you.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. When your first run ends, the game asks whether ads may be personalised. If you agree, Google may use your phone's advertising identifier. If you don't, ads still show, without it. The game plays the same either way.
  • The game sends anonymous usage statistics (such as runs played) through Aptabase. They contain nothing that identifies you or your phone.
  • We don't sell data, and we receive no personal data about you.

Who we are

Hill Roller is made by Semih Masat, an independent developer ("we", "us"). For any question about privacy, write to semihmasat@outlook.com.

What the game keeps on your phone

Hill Roller saves your points, the hills and rollers you own, upgrades, best distances and times, stars, quests and your settings (such as tilt or touch steering) in the app's own storage on your phone. This stays on your phone, and in your phone's own backups if you use them. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

The game doesn't use your precise location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.

Ads by Google AdMob

Hill Roller shows a banner after a run and on the pause screen, never while you're rolling. You can also choose to watch a video ad to make up points you're short of, or to keep a run's points when you end it from the pause screen. These ads come from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google Ireland Limited).

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the game collects:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow it (see below), and identifiers limited to this app or this developer's apps.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate location (country, city or region) can be worked out. Not your precise location.
  • Advertising data: which ads were shown, and whether you tapped or watched them.
  • Use of the app: for example when the app was opened and how long an ad was on screen.
  • Diagnostics: crash and performance data about the ads software.
  • Device information: such as phone model, iOS version, language and screen size.

Google uses this data to show ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ads, to detect fraud and abuse, and, if you agree, to personalise ads. How Google uses data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's privacy policy.

From Google we only receive totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned. We receive nothing that identifies you.

Your choices about ads

The game asks once, when your first run ends:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks whether Google may store and use data for personalised ads and related purposes. You can agree, refuse, or choose per purpose. If you refuse, Google shows "limited ads": they aren't personalised, and Google uses your IP address and device information only to deliver them and fight fraud, which can include storing fraud-detection data on your phone. See Google's explanation of limited ads.
  • On every iPhone, a short explanation is followed by Apple's question whether Hill Roller may track you. If you choose "Allow", Google may use your advertising identifier to personalise and measure ads across apps. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google doesn't get the advertising identifier and ads show without it.

You can change your answers at any time:

  • Google's consent form: the game's Settings → Privacy choices (shown where the form applies).
  • Apple's question: your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Hill Roller.
  • How Google personalises ads for you: My Ad Center.

The banners can't be switched off. The video ads only play when you choose them.

Anonymous usage statistics (Aptabase)

To see how the game is played and what to improve, the game sends short usage events to Aptabase: for example that a run started or ended, on which hill and with which roller, that something was bought with points, or that a video ad was watched. Each event comes with the game's version, the iOS version, the phone model and the language.

  • No identifiers: no advertising identifier, no device identifier, no account, no cookies.
  • A random session number groups the events of one session. It is made fresh each time and is never stored on your phone.
  • Your IP address is used only to work out your country and region, and is not stored.
  • The statistics are stored by Aptabase in the European Union (Germany) and kept for up to five years.

Because nothing in them identifies you or your phone, these statistics can't be linked to you, and aren't used for ads.

Apple's App Analytics

If you chose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers), Apple gives us anonymous totals, such as installs, sessions and crashes. You can turn this off there.

Children

Hill Roller is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we'll help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe and the UK, or US state privacy laws), you have the right to ask what data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to its use and to withdraw consent. We hold no personal data about you. For data Google holds, use the choices above or Google's privacy tools; if you need help, write to us. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

The legal basis for personalised ads is your consent. For limited or non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and the anonymous usage statistics, it is our legitimate interest in running and improving a free game paid for by ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change matters for your privacy, the game will tell you.
