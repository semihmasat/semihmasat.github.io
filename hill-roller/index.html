Hill Roller

Roll a tyre down real mountains. A free downhill game for iPhone.

Get help

Something not working, or an idea? Write to semihmasat@outlook.com. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what happened. We usually answer within a few days.

How to play

  • Steer by tilting your phone, or by holding a finger on the left or right of the screen. Switch between them in Settings (the gear on the home screen).
  • Brake with the pedal at the bottom of the screen.
  • Points come from distance, jumps, stars, reaching the finish and quests. Spend them on new hills, rollers, upgrades and hill extras.
  • Hidden rollers wait somewhere on seven of the hills. Roll into one to keep it. The STAR MAP hill extra shows where it is.
  • Secret hills: two hills can't be bought. Find their maps on other hills.

Questions

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Only on your phone. There's no account and no cloud save. Your progress comes along when you move to a new iPhone from a backup (iCloud or your computer). Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Why does the game ask about ads and tracking after my first run?

The game is free and paid for by ads. When your first run ends, it asks once whether the ads may be personalised: Google's consent form in Europe, the UK and Switzerland, and Apple's tracking question on every iPhone. Ads show either way, and the game plays the same whatever you answer.

How do I change my answer?

For Google's consent form, open the game's Settings and tap Privacy choices. For Apple's question, go to your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the privacy policy for more.

The video ad button doesn't work.

Video ads need an internet connection, and sometimes there's no ad to show. Try again in a minute. Runs themselves don't need a connection.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhones with iOS 15 or later. It runs best on an iPhone from the last few years.

Privacy

No accounts, and nothing that identifies you is sent to us. Ads are from Google AdMob; anonymous usage statistics from Aptabase. Read the privacy policy.
